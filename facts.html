How provenance works

Lineage
Medallion lineage. Raw instrument and literature data lands in bronze. Cleaning and provenance attachment happens in silver. Only benchmark-ready, fully-attributed records reach gold.
Method codes
100% of rows carry a method code.
Quarantine
Records with missing or unknown methods quarantine automatically: they are never silently mixed into your analysis.
Benchmarks
Each dataset ships with a sealed, falsifiable benchmark. Predict first, measure later, score against lab-grade truth.
Versioning
Every release gets a version and a DOI. The version never changes under you.

An honest note on V1: the current seeds are synthetic and labeled as such on every row. Real-data ingestion is underway. We would rather tell you that plainly than let you discover it.
