CLIMATESHIFT-1 trial sites, on the map

Every v1 hemp trial record in CLIMATESHIFT-1, plotted at its trial site. Click a point for the full record: cultivar, yield, THC and CBD panel, lab accreditation, and the compliance flag. The viewer is GeoLibre v3.5.2 (MIT), self-hosted on this portal, running in read-only viewer mode.

Open the viewer full screen if the embedded frame is tight on your device. Working offline or on a restricted network? The same records ship as a self-contained project file with the data embedded and a blank basemap, so the layer loads with no tile service at all.

What you are looking at

The layer is generated from the dataset's own seed records (geo/tools/build_geojson.py in this repo, source seed in lbt-os). The seed records name trial locations rather than coordinates, so each point sits at the city coordinate of its named site. Measurement values are the records' own, unmodified. V1 rows are synthetic and labeled as such, here and everywhere on the portal.

  • Corvallis, OR: Oregon State, 6 records
